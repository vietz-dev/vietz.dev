# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who investigate `vietz.dev` or Justin's `@vietz.dev` email address and want to find the person behind them. Visitors can also read about his work, projects, and writing.

## Product Purpose

This is Justin Vietz's personal website. It gives his domain and email address an associated public presence and lets visitors learn about him. It is not a job search site: Justin is employed and is not looking to change jobs.

## Positioning

A first-person portfolio at Justin's own domain, connected to the email identity he uses.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive after seeing a `@vietz.dev` email address or the domain. The site offers an introduction, projects, a blog, a CV, and links to Justin's public profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present verified facts about Justin and his work without implying that he is available for a new role.
- Profile facts must come from Justin or content already on the site; keep their duplicated copies consistent.
- Keep the German legal pages in German. Confirm legal wording with Justin before changing it.
- Prefer self-hosted assets; new third-party requests require a privacy disclosure.
- The site is an Astro 7 project.
- DevProxy is a second project. Its planned public address is `devproxy.vietz.dev`, but it is not hosted yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and domain: Justin Vietz and `vietz.dev`.
- The site uses the personal “Moin” greeting and existing first-person voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing introduction and profile: `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/pages/cv.astro`, `public/llms.txt`.
- Existing WorkTime listing: `src/pages/projects.astro`; live product: `https://worktime.vietz.dev`.
- DevProxy documentation: `/Users/justinv/Workspace/devproxy/docs/content/docs/`. It documents a CLI that gives development services in Git worktrees predictable local HTTPS URLs and manages their routing through Caddy.
- Existing writing: `src/content/blog/`.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors identify Justin and understand the purpose of the domain quickly.
- Show actual work and writing with enough context to be useful.
- Keep contact information findable without suggesting job availability.
- Preserve factual accuracy across every profile copy.
